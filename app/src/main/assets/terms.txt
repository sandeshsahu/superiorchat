1. WHAT THIS APPLICATION IS

Superior Chat is a free, open-source, high-privacy stealth messaging application built specifically for couples and close partners who require a discreet, serverless communication channel. It uses the official Telegram Bot API strictly as a transport layer and is NOT affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

The app has no backend server of its own. Messages travel exclusively between your device and the Telegram servers using your own Bot Token.

4. SECURITY LIMITATIONS & ACCEPTED RISKS

Superior Chat is designed to protect against a specific threat model: casual snoopers, nosy individuals, or people who briefly access your unlocked phone. It is NOT designed to protect against forensic experts or technically skilled attackers with prolonged physical access to your unlocked device. If such a person has your unlocked device, they can bypass stealth measures.

QR Code Setup Risk: By default, your setup QR code is protected by the Two-Step PIN mechanism. If you choose "Direct Mode" (no PIN), your Bot Token is encrypted only with a hardcoded key that is publicly visible in the open-source code. Anyone who obtains your Direct Mode QR code can decrypt it and gain full access to your bot — reading your messages and sending on your behalf. You accept this risk if you choose that option.

Group Chat Exposure Risk: You must only communicate with your bot in a private 1-on-1 Telegram chat. Adding the bot to a group chat or public channel will instantly expose all your private messages to everyone in that group. You assume full responsibility for securing the bot's Telegram environment.

5. WEBRTC CALLS — CRITICAL RISKS

If you use the voice/video calling feature, you must understand and accept the following:

IP ADDRESS EXPOSURE: WebRTC is Peer-to-Peer by default. Both call participants can observe each other's public IP address. A TURN relay server is required for full IP anonymity — by default, none is configured.

THIRD-PARTY INFRASTRUCTURE RISK: The default WebRTC signaling pages and servers are provided strictly as a convenience for testing. Using the maintainer's default servers, or any unknown third-party server, is STRICTLY NOT RECOMMENDED for privacy-critical use. The server owner can inject code to steal your call secrets, log your activity, or compromise your privacy. The developer assumes NO RESPONSIBILITY for any privacy breaches caused by third-party infrastructure. For guaranteed security, you MUST self-host your own WebRTC pages and signaling server.

FAKE VIDEO MUTE: When you tap "Mute Video" in a call, only the UI hides the video. The camera hardware continues to capture and transmit video to the other participant. This is a known limitation due to PeerJS constraints. You accept this risk.
